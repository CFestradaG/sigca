## Brand & Style

The design system embodies emotional security, clinical clarity, and nurturing warmth for a mental health and listening & spoken language center in Guatemala. The visual language purposefully avoids the cold, sterile conventions of traditional hospitals, establishing instead a welcoming domestic-clinical hybrid rooted in gentle organic balance.

- **Tone and Voice:** Warm, reassuring, deeply professional, and family-centered. Interactions feel human, patient, and grounding.
- **Design Style:** Soft Organic Modernism with subtle tactile comfort. Generous breathing room, gentle surface elevations, and smooth rounded geometry soften clinical anxiety for parents, children, and adult patients.
- **Target Audience:** Parents seeking auditory-verbal therapy for their children, individuals navigating psychological care, and multidisciplinary healthcare professionals across Guatemala.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between grounding earthen neutrals and calming aquatic botanicals, accented with an affectionate coral for decisive calls-to-action.

- **Primary Teal (`#2A9D8F`):** Anchors healing, auditory stimulation, progress, and primary interactive cues. Also functions as the positive state indicator.
- **Secondary Coral (`#E76F51`):** Reserved for high-priority appointments, critical reassurance accents, badges, and attention-guiding micro-actions.
- **Deep Slate Navy (`#1F3A4D`):** Applied to primary typography, icons, and structured headings to ensure assertive readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Muted Slate (`#5C7180`):** Designed for secondary copy, metadata, timestamps, and subtle field instructions.
- **Base Sand Canvas (`#F4EFE6`):** The default root canvas tint, evoking natural warmth and tactile paper tranquility.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#FAF7F2`):** Pure white is reserved for high-priority interactive cards and form canvases, while `#FAF7F2` acts as nested surface layering.
- **Subtle Dividers (`#DFD7CA`):** Neutral hairline partitions with low visual vibration.
- **Accessibility:** All body pairings against light sand and white backgrounds exceed WCAG AA ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text, 7:1 for headers).

## Typography

The typography pairs **Nunito Sans** for titles with **Inter** for dense interface data and clinical text.

- **Nunito Sans:** The rounded terminal geometry removes institutional austerity, creating an approachable, reassuring envelope for therapy descriptions, patient welcomes, and section landmarks.
- **Inter:** Provides maximum legibility, clean x-height, and neutral optical rhythm for assessment reports, intake forms, and reading comprehension.
- **Monetary Units:** Currency displays use `label-md` or `label-lg` with the Guatemalan Quetzal glyph (`Q`) bound tightly without a line break (e.g., `Q 450.00`). Tabular figures (`tnum`) must be enforced for fee schedules and clinical session timers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a fluid 12-column responsive grid underpinned by generous vertical whitespace to mitigate sensory overload and reduce cognitive fatigue for stressed parents.

- **Container Widths:** Main desktop layout restricts maximum reading widths to `1200px` for portals and `720px` for focused narrative or clinical form passages.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, `margin` of `3rem`, `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Multi-pane views for schedule/booking and specialist directory.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Two-column forms collapse to single-column blocks.
  - **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Fixed bottom bars anchor emergency booking actions; content panels stack vertically.
- **Whitespace Rules:** Component internal paddings default to `space-lg` (24px) for cards and modals to preserve physical comfort and clarity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting and soft tonal separation rather than sharp drop shadows or cold glassmorphism.

- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Shadows carry an organic slate-sand undertone rather than pure grayscale:
  - *Resting Surface (Cards):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.02)`.
  - *Interactive Hover:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.03)`.
  - *Floating Dialogues / Menus:* `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 58, 77, 0.04)`.
- **Tonal Layers:** Deepest ground sits at `#F4EFE6`. Interactive working surfaces stack atop it in `#FFFFFF` with hairline borders (`#DFD7CA`), maintaining tactile separation without harsh contrast borders.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`) to maintain a friendly, human aesthetic.

- **Base Corner Radii:** Buttons, input fields, badges, and chips use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Surface Radii:** Cards, dialogue sheets, banners, and panel groupings use `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Tactile Softness:** Elements interacting directly with user touch points avoid sharp corners, conveying safety and approachable care.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum height of 48px (never below 44px) on both mobile and desktop.
- **Primary Action:** Solid Teal (`#2A9D8F`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle hover lift and color shift to `#238276`.
- **Accent / Urgent Action:** Soft Coral (`#E76F51`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, used exclusively for primary calls to action like booking and consultation requests.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1.5px solid border in `#2A9D8F`, text `#2A9D8F`, hover state fills with an ultra-light tint (`rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.08)`).
- **Padding:** 14px vertical, 24px horizontal. Corner radius: `12px`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- **Base Architecture:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#DFD7CA`, corner radius `12px`, padding 12px 16px, height 48px.
- **States:** Active/Focus introduces a 2px outer glow (`rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.2)`) and shifts border to `#2A9D8F`. Error state uses `#E76F51` with supportive, empathetic validation microcopy below the field.
- **Labels:** Always persistent and visible above the field in `#1F3A4D` (`label-md`). Helper text uses `#5C7180` (`body-sm`).

### Cards & Patient Enclosures
- **Treatment & Specialist Cards:** Canvas `#FFFFFF`, hairline border 1px solid `#DFD7CA`, ambient resting shadow, corner radius `16px`, padding `24px`.
- **Quiet Info Banners:** Background `#FAF7F2`, left accent border 4px solid `#2A9D8F`, no shadow, corner radius `12px`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height 32px, radius 16px, padding 4px 12px.
- **Auditory & Psychological Categories:** Pill-shaped, soft background tint (`rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.1)` for teal or `rgba(231, 111, 81, 0.1)` for coral), font `label-sm` matching the parent hue.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Touch Target:** Enclosed within a 44px hit-box. Visual selector 20px x 20px with a 6px radius for checkboxes and full circle for radios.
- **Selected State:** Fill `#2A9D8F` with clean white SVG checkmark. Border `#2A9D8F`.

### Appointment & Fee Cards (Quetzales)
- Clear financial breakdowns featuring fees prefixed with `Q` in `#1F3A4D` bold tabular figures.
- Direct contact triggers embedded in cards with clear indicators for in-person clinic visits versus tele-therapy sessions.
